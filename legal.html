Privacy Policy

Summary

JEE Question Tracker is a local-first study app. Your study data stays on your device unless you choose to turn on cloud sync. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

Your subjects, notebooks, question sets, question statuses, topic tags, reference books, My Day targets and checklist, settings (theme, layout, reminder times, daily target), the name you enter, and the hashes of your PIN and recovery code (never the PIN itself). All of this is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device.

Optional cloud sync

If you sign in with Google, the app uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. This gives the app your Google account ID, name and email address, and stores a copy of your tracker data under your account so it can sync across your devices and offer a previous version. Google processes this under its own privacy policy. If you never sign in, nothing is sent to any server. Signing out stops syncing; the copy already in the cloud stays until it is deleted (see Your choices).

What we do not do

We do not show ads, run analytics, sell or share your data, or profile you. The only third-party service is Google Firebase, and only when you sign in.

Sharing and email

Share cards, messages and email reports are handled by your device's share sheet or email app. They are not sent to us. What you share goes only where you choose to send it.

Notifications

Reminders use notifications on your device if you allow them. There is no push server and no data leaves your device for this.

Security

Your PIN and recovery code are stored as one-way hashes. App Lock keeps casual users out of the app screen but is not encryption of your stored data, so keep your device secure and export backups regularly.

Your choices

You can export your data at any time, delete everything with Reset Tracker Data, erase all app data from the lock screen, or clear your browser's site data. To remove your cloud copy and sign-in record, contact us at [add your contact email here] from the email on your Google account and we will delete it.

Children

The app is made for students. It is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please use it with a parent or guardian.
